Our process

Nothing goes in the machine until we’ve looked at it.

Every item goes through the same four steps.

  1. Step 1

    Inspect

    Each piece is checked by hand: care label, fabric, stains, loose buttons and small tears. Anything unusual is noted before cleaning starts.

  2. Step 2

    Clean

    Washed or dry cleaned with a method and products chosen for that fabric, never a one-size-fits-all cycle.

  3. Step 3

    Finish

    Steam pressed and finished by hand for sharp collars and clean creases, without shine or scorch marks.

  4. Step 4

    Return

    Collect from your outlet when it's ready. Need it fast? Hand it over by 12 noon and Express has it ready the same evening.

Services

Everything your clothes need.

Not sure which service you need? Bring it in and we’ll advise.

Washing

Premium detergents and fabric-safe care that keep everyday clothes fresh, soft and long-lasting.

Dry cleaning

For suits, silks and designer pieces. Removes tough stains while protecting colour, texture and shape.

Pressing

Steam pressing and hand finishing for sharp collars, clean creases and a shop-new look.

Stain removal

Curry, tea, oil, ink and make-up stains treated to suit the fabric, so the colour isn’t harmed.

Express service

Hand it over by 12 noon and it’s ready the same day after 6.00pm, at the same standard. Ideal when you’re travelling.

Urgent pressing

For last-minute meetings and events. Garments pressed and ready in a short turnaround. Terms apply.
