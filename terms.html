What to know before you hand over your garments to LaundroPlus (Pvt) Ltd.

01Before you hand over

  • Please check all pockets and linen before handing items to our counters or pickup vehicles. LaundroPlus cannot take responsibility for valuables left inside.
  • Please tell our staff about any existing damage, stains or discolouration. LaundroPlus (Pvt) Ltd is not responsible or liable for any loss or damage arising from defects that were not declared.

02Cleaning and risk

  • LaundroPlus (Pvt) Ltd accepts all laundry, dry cleaning and pressing at the owner’s risk. We take the utmost care with every garment.
  • Spot and stain removal is done at the customer’s risk.
  • Some hard stains cannot be removed in the normal process, as that would need stronger chemicals that can harm the fabric. If we cannot remove a stain, a stain tag is attached to the piece.
  • LaundroPlus (Pvt) Ltd is not responsible or liable for shrinkage, colour fastness, lost buttons or belts, slack elastic, ornaments, anything left in the garments, or water-based stains after dry cleaning.

03Express and urgent services

  • One-day, express and urgent services carry an extra charge on the standard price: One-day +25%, Express +50%, Urgent +100%.
  • Express orders are accepted between 8.00am and 12 noon and are ready the same day after 6.00pm.
  • Urgent service is for pressing only. It is accepted until 5.30pm at our Cinnamon Grand outlet and is ready 3 hours after hand-over.
  • Ready times are a guide. Our team confirms the exact time when you hand over your garments.

04Collecting your order

  • Please collect your garments within 7 days of the order’s dispatch date. LaundroPlus (Pvt) Ltd is not responsible for garments after 7 days from dispatch.
  • If an order is not collected or delivered within 60 days of dispatch, we will inform you and then dispose of the items or donate them to a charity programme.
  • To change an order, please contact our hotline, our WhatsApp number or the relevant outlet.

05Claims, damage and redos

  • Every garment goes through a quality check. If you are not satisfied with the washing, dry cleaning or pressing, please ask for a redo within 24 hours of collection.
  • Please report any lost or damaged garments within 24 hours of collection, together with the bill.
  • Our liability for loss or damage is limited to the fair value of the item when it was received, allowing for age, fashion and use, and will not exceed 5 times the charge for that item.
  • Claims may be settled with money or new goods. We may ask for a receipt or other proof of the purchase price; without valid proof, the compensation value may be affected.
  • Approved claims are settled within 30 days of us being informed. If LaundroPlus pays for a damaged item, we have the right to keep it.

06Offers and vouchers

  • Offers, discounts, vouchers and coupons are valid only for the dates stated in the relevant promotion.
